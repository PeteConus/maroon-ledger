What's the difference between borrowing and net debt?

Borrowing is the total the government owes: mostly loans from Queensland Treasury Corporation, plus leases and similar arrangements.

Net debt subtracts the cash, deposits, investments and advances the government holds. Queensland holds large investments (much of it set aside for public servants' superannuation), so net debt is well below borrowing.

The General Government Sector is the core of government: departments and agencies funded mainly by taxes and grants. The Non-financial Public Sector adds government-owned businesses such as the energy, water and port corporations, whose borrowing pays for their own assets.
