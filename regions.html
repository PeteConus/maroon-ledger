Reading this page

These are capital purchases (buildings, roads, equipment and other non-financial assets) planned for 2026–27 by Queensland Government entities, including government-owned corporations. They are not operating spending, and they do not include capital grants paid to councils and others. The “Queensland Treasury” line includes the government-owned energy, water and port businesses, which is why regions hosting large energy projects rank high. Treasury spreads its contingency reserve across regions in proportion to each region's capital spend. Per-resident figures divide by the ABS estimated resident population at 30 June 2025. Regions follow the ABS Statistical Area Level 4 boundaries: “Cairns” is the Cairns SA4, which takes in Cairns, the Cassowary Coast, Port Douglas and the Daintree, and the eastern Tablelands; Cape York, the Torres Strait and the Gulf sit in “Queensland – Outback”.
