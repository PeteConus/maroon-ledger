Portfolios.

Each department's own Budget statement: what it spends on each service, how many people it employs, and how it measured up against the targets it set.

Why these don't add up to the Budget total. Departmental figures come from each portfolio's Service Delivery Statement. They include payments between agencies and exclude amounts a department administers on behalf of the State, so adding them up does not give the $105.8bn of General Government expenses. Read them department by department. Every statement shows three columns: the 2025–26 Budget, the 2025–26 estimated actual and the 2026–27 Budget; there are no projections beyond 2026–27.
