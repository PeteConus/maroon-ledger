About The Maroon Ledger.

A state Budget says a lot about a state's priorities. Understanding it shouldn't take an economics degree, or a weekend with a stack of Budget papers.

The Maroon Ledger brings Queensland's published Budget figures together in a form you can follow: where the money comes from, where it goes, what has changed, how forecasts have tracked against outcomes, and where the State plans to build.

Independent, with the sources in view

This is an independent project. It is not a Queensland Government publication and is not affiliated with any political party. We use official published sources and link back to them. We explain what the figures mean and leave readers to form their own views about the choices behind them.

Some distinctions matter, so we keep them visible. Revenue and expenses are not the same as cash received and paid. An estimate is not an outcome. A change in a number does not, by itself, say why it changed. Where the published information doesn't support a deeper answer, we say so.

Corrections

If you spot an error or something that is unclear, please tell us which page, financial year and line you mean, so we can check it against the source.

Privacy

This version runs entirely from files on your own computer. It sends nothing anywhere, other than loading two web fonts if you are online. It remembers the financial year you last chose in your browser's local storage, on your device only.
